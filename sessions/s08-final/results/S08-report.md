## Which project conclusions stand

The proxy-error scaling anomaly stands and is the durable result. It survived the OHLC rebuild, the calendar exclusion, effective sub-bar count and both filter revisions, moving by at most 1.6e-04 in S06R; a positive control recovered the trigamma reference through the identical code path while no synthetic arm reproduced the observed flatness; and SPY replicates it at two venues under both sampling conventions. K2 is reported above on repaired losses as DOES NOT FIRE. The reliability programme now has an estimator that respects its own bound almost everywhere: the intercept route violates it at 4 of 128 grid points and only by about one percent, through fit residual rather than through the estimator itself, against 14 violations for E2 on the same grid, and it is reported beside E2 and E4 rather than replacing them. RGARCH's behaviour at intraday GLOBEX horizons is a failure of this implementation, not of Realized GARCH. The holdout remains closed and opens once, at the economic validation of the sizing consequence.
