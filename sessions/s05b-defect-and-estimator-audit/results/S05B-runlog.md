## Constants used

- Sampling variance reference: `trigamma(M/2)` = `scipy.special.polygamma(1, M/2)` throughout; `2/M` computed and reported alongside but never used as the reference.
- Truncation: 3 local standard deviations, threshold `3*sqrt(BV/M)` (Mancini), matching S05 Part A's TRQ3_TRV3.
- Boundary trim for the separation fit: first and last 5 one-minute returns of each window.
- B1 boundary minutes (inherited from S05 Part B): NY 09:30, 09:31, 15:59, 16:00, 18:01.
- Realized kernel bandwidth: H = 0.97 * xi^(4/5) * n^(3/5), xi^2 = omega^2/IV, omega^2 from S03 N1 (DECISIONS item 16).
- Two-scale K = c* n^(2/3), c* = (12 omega^4 / IQ)^(1/3) (Zhang-Mykland-Ait-Sahalia 2005).
- Sub-bar construction: sub-bar j spans one-minute return indices [floor(j*L/M), floor((j+1)*L/M)).
- Elasticity fit: OLS of log((1-lambda)/lambda) on log M, grid points with lambda outside (0,1) dropped and counted.
- No RNG is used anywhere in S05B; every phase is deterministic.
